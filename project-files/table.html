How it works

A weekly record of your weight, so you can see what changed and how close you are to your goal.

What this app does

Each week you log your weight. The app saves that entry, compares it with the week before, and rolls every entry into a summary. When a new weight is lower than the last one, a short celebration plays.

How to use it
  1. Set a goal. Open Settings and choose Edit. Type the weight you want to reach. A goal is required before you can add an entry.
  2. Log once a week. Enter the date, your current weight, and an optional comment, then choose Submit Form.
  3. Read the table. Each row is one entry. “Weight Lost Since Last Week” is last week’s weight minus this week’s. A positive number is weight lost. A negative number is weight gained. The first entry is 0.0.
  4. Check the summary. It shows how many weeks passed from your first entry to your latest, your average weekly change, total weight lost, how much is left, the percent of the goal you have reached, and your goal weight.
  5. Find an old entry. Search by date or by weight. Choose Get All Rows Back to restore the full list.
  6. Fix a mistake. Delete removes one entry after you confirm.
The top bar

Scroll To Table, Weight-Loss Statistics, Search, and View Latest Entry jump to those parts of the page. Settings also holds the light and dark theme and the celebration sound. Use Ask something in the bottom right to open the diet assistant. The icon next to your name logs you out.

Good luck! Enjoy your journey, stay consistent, and lose the weight one week at a time.
